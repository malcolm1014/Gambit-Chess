Gambit Chess

Classic strategy, redefined by modern mechanics.

Welcome to Gambit Chess. This is not your standard game of chess. Gambit Chess introduces RPG-style stat allocation, a resource economy, and hidden information mechanics wrapped in a sleek, modern 3D interface.

Why Gambit Chess is Different:

  • Combat Thresholds: Standard movement applies, but you can only capture an enemy piece if your Total Offense mathematically exceeds their Total Defense.
  • Pre-Game Allocation: Roll a Wolfram Rule 30 generated dice pool to secretly allocate stat modifiers (+Offense or +Defense) to your pieces before the game begins.
  • Gambit Points (GP): Earn GP when you sacrifice pieces. Use your GP economy to activate special cards.
  • Hidden Traps: Spend GP to place hidden traps on your side of the board. Enemy pieces are destroyed instantly upon stepping on them.
