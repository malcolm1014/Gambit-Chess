What makes it different

Four twists layered on top of standard chess.

🎲

Stat Allocation

Before move one, roll 6d8 three times and keep the best. Secretly spend those points as +Offense or +Defense on your pieces.

⚔️

Combat Threshold

A piece can only capture when its Offense ≥ the enemy's Defense. A well-buffed pawn can shrug off a queen.

💰

Gambit-Point Economy

Lose a piece and you're paid in Gambit Points equal to its value. Spend them to empower survivors — sacrifice becomes strategy.

🪤

Hidden Traps

Spend points to bury hidden traps on your half of the board. Any enemy that wanders onto one is destroyed on the spot.

How a game flows

Learn it in about a minute.

  1. Allocate. Each side rolls for a points pool and distributes +Offense / +Defense across its army, then reveals.
  2. Maneuver. Move like normal chess — but every capture must clear the Combat Threshold.
  3. Bank & spend. Captured pieces earn you Gambit Points; cash them in to empower pieces or set traps.
  4. Checkmate. The King has Infinite Defense and can't be captured — so corner it the classic way to win.
