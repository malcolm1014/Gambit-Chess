Chess, with stats, sacrifices and traps.

Gambit Chess keeps the board you know and adds RPG-style stats. Before the game you roll dice and power up your pieces. A capture only works if your attacker is strong enough. Losing pieces earns you Gambit Points, and you spend those on cards and hidden traps.

How to play, in 4 steps

The game is in two phases. First you set up your army, then you play.

1

Pick a mode

1 Player (vs AI) puts you on White against the computer. 2 Player (Local) lets two people take turns on one screen.

2

Roll & spend your dice

Click 🎲 Roll Dice. The game rolls 6d8 three times and you keep the highest total, and you can re-roll before you start. Use the + / − buttons to add Offense or Defense to each piece type, then click Play Game. Any points you leave unspent are assigned automatically.

3

Move & capture

White moves first. Click one of your pieces, then click the square you want. If a capture isn't strong enough, the game tells you why and the move is cancelled.

4

Use your GP

Your GP is shown at the top. Click a card you can afford. For Pitfall, click an empty square on your half of the board to hide a trap there.

The rules

Pieces move like they do in chess. The difference is in combat and the resource economy.

⚔ The Combat Threshold

Each piece has an Offense score and a Defense score. Each one is the piece's base value plus whatever points you put on it during setup. A capture only goes through if:

Attacker Offense (base + mod) ≥ Defender Defense (base + mod)

That means a well-built pawn can take a knight, and a queen you made heavily defensive can be very hard to remove.

🎲 Allocation Phase

Each side rolls 6d8 three times and keeps the best total, which comes to somewhere between 6 and 48 points. The dice use a Wolfram Rule 30 cellular-automaton random number generator. Points go to a piece type, so +2 Offense on Pawn gives all eight pawns +2 Offense.

In 2-player mode, White allocates first and Black allocates second. Have your opponent look away while you set yours.

💰 Gambit Points (the sacrifice economy)

  • White starts with 10 GP and Black starts with 0.
  • When one of your pieces is captured, you get GP equal to its base value. Losing material pays you.
  • You spend GP on Gambit Cards and traps.

💣 Hidden traps

  • A trap costs 4 GP (it's the Pitfall card).
  • You can only place one on your half of the board, and only one trap per square.
  • When an enemy piece lands on it, the piece is destroyed and the trap is used up. Your own pieces can stand on it safely.

⚙ Movement in the current version

  • Pawns move one square forward and capture one square diagonally. A pawn that reaches the far side is promoted to a Queen and keeps its bonuses. There's no two-square first move (that's what the Sprint card is for) and no en passant.
  • Knights, bishops, rooks and queens move as in chess. Only knights jump over pieces.
  • Kings move one square in any direction. There's no castling.
  • Check & checkmate: the King's Defense is infinite, so it can't be captured. Just like chess, you win by checkmate, and you can't make a move that leaves your own King in check.
  • Other endings: if a King steps on an enemy trap, it's destroyed and that side loses. A side with no legal moves that isn't in check is a stalemate (draw). 50 moves each without a capture is also a draw.

Strategy tips

Some ideas to get you started.

♟

Points on pawns go far

A point on a piece type applies to every piece of that type. +1 on Pawn upgrades eight pieces, while +1 on Queen upgrades one.

🛡

Out-defend their attackers

If your rook's Defense is higher than every Offense your opponent has, nothing can capture it.

⚔

Know your kill lines

Write down which of your pieces can beat which of theirs. A failed capture costs you nothing, so feel free to test.

💰

Trade on purpose

Every piece you lose gives you GP. A well-timed sacrifice can pay for a trap in the right spot.

💣

Trap the entry squares

Put Pitfalls on the squares enemy knights and rooks need to reach to invade your side.

♚

White's 10 GP head start

As White, you can afford two traps before Black has earned anything. Use them early.

FAQ

Do I need to download or sign up for anything?

No. Gambit Chess runs entirely in your browser and is free to play.

The board is blank or won't load.

The game needs WebGL for its 3D board. Use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. If it still doesn't show, try opening it in its own tab with the "Open full screen" button.

Why was my capture rejected?

Your attacker's total Offense was lower than the target's total Defense. The red message above the board shows both numbers.

"Play Game" is greyed out.

Roll the dice first: click 🎲 Roll Dice. After that, Play Game is always available, and any unspent points are assigned for you.

Why can't I make that move?

Your King is in check, or the move would put it in check. Read the red message above the board. It tells you exactly why the move was refused.

How do I restart?

Click New Game above the board, or Play Again when a game ends. Each new game starts with a fresh dice roll.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, but there's a lot on screen, so a tablet, laptop or desktop works best.
