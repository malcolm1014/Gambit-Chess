How to play Gambit Chess

1 · Allocation. Each side rolls 6d8 three times and keeps the highest sum. Spend those points as +O (Offense) or +D (Defense) on your pieces, then confirm. Pass the device between players.

2 · Combat Threshold. A piece may capture an enemy only if its Total Offense ≥ the enemy's Total Defense. Base values: Pawn 1, Knight/Bishop 3, Rook 5, Queen 9, King ∞.

3 · Gambit Points. When your piece is captured you gain GP equal to its base value. Spend GP: Empower (+1 O and +1 D to one piece, 3 GP) or Set Trap (hidden token on your half, 2 GP — an enemy that lands on it is destroyed).

4 · Winning. The King has Infinite Defense, so it can never be captured — but check & checkmate work like normal chess, so corner the enemy King to win. (A trap destroys even a King.)
